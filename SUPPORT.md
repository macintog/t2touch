# Support and continuation

t2touch is being prepared as a research handoff for engineers who want to reproduce
and integrate Linux-native T2 Touch ID adoption. The native proof of concept is not
yet published.

The author is not taking on ongoing maintenance. Bug fixes, compatibility updates,
integration work, pull-request review, and response times are not promised. The
repository must not be presented as a supported authentication product.

After delivery, the author may answer clarification questions about the published
work. That does not establish a support service or a commitment to make changes.
The channel and duration have not been selected. No automatic archive date is set.

Forks and downstream integration are welcome under the applicable licenses. A project
that adopts the work owns its integration, validation, releases, and user support.
Do not send t2touch questions to another project's maintainers unless they have agreed
to receive them.

Future local refinements may be handed to another project as discrete contributions.
They do not change this maintenance boundary. Archiving the repository will be an
explicit decision after delivery; the source will remain available for reuse.

The inherited [security policy](SECURITY.md) describes upstream's existing software.
It does not establish a private reporting service or response commitment for t2touch.
Before the native release, its security guidance must be reviewed for the actual
publication and maintenance model.
